TimeStamp!

Guess when it happened.

A photo from your own camera roll pops up with the date hidden. Guess the year, then the month, then the exact day. The closer you get, the more you score.

  • A new Daily Challenge every day, with leaderboards
  • Party Mode: play live with up to 10 friends
  • Streaks, badges, and a solo practice mode

Free · iPhone

Draftable

Who's got the best taste? Prove it.

Pick any category, from the greatest NBA players to the best fast food, and draft your picks live with friends. Then send it to the group chat and let everyone vote on who drafted best.

  • Classic snake draft, or Moneyball with a $20 budget
  • Friends vote from a link, with no app needed
  • Build your own boards and set your own prices

Nametags

The bar is the game board.

Your crew gets secret quests that get you talking to strangers: get someone to say a certain word, or find someone with a twin. Log who helped you, call BS on your friends, and race for bounties until last call.

  • Friends nearby join with one tap. No codes.
  • Two ways to play: Classic, or Shoot Your Shot
  • Local quests for San Francisco, Los Angeles, and New York
