Data Deletion

Andrew Maxwell is committed to minimal data collection. Here's exactly what each app stores and how to remove it.

TimeStamp!

Available Now
What TimeStamp! Stores TimeStamp! does not upload your photos or store personal data on any server. The only external data is anonymous usage analytics (Firebase) and optional Game Center leaderboard scores (Apple). No account is created.
1

Revoke Photo Access

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → TimeStamp! and set access to None. The app will no longer be able to read any photo data from your device.

2

Delete the App

Long-press the TimeStamp! icon on your home screen → Remove App → Delete App. This removes the app and all locally stored data (settings, cached scores) from your device.

3

Remove Game Center Data (Optional)

If you participated in the Daily Challenge leaderboard, your scores are stored by Apple under your Game Center account. To manage or delete this data, visit Settings → [Your Name] → Game Center and sign out, or contact Apple Support to remove Game Center data.

4

Analytics (Automatic)

Anonymous Firebase Analytics data is not linked to your identity and cannot be individually deleted. It is retained according to Google's standard retention schedule and automatically expires.

Draftable

Available Now
✓ Auto-deleted after 3 hours
What Draftable Stores Player names, draft picks, vote choices, and room data are stored temporarily in Firebase Realtime Database while a game is active. All data is automatically and permanently deleted 3 hours after the room is created. No accounts are created and no data persists beyond that window.
1

Wait — Data Auto-Deletes After 3 Hours

In most cases, you don't need to do anything. All room data, picks, and votes are automatically and permanently purged from Firebase 3 hours after the room was created. Nothing persists after that point.

2

Request Immediate Deletion

If you need a specific game's data deleted before the 3-hour window, email us at support@timestampgames.com with the subject line "Data Deletion — Draftable" and include the room code or poll link URL. We will delete the data manually within 24 hours.

3

Delete the App

Long-press the Draftable icon → Remove App → Delete App. Since Draftable stores no data locally beyond app state, deleting the app removes everything on-device. Any active room data in Firebase will still auto-expire after 3 hours.

Need help?

Email us for any data requests — we respond within 1–3 business days.
