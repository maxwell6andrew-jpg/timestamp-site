Accessibility

Last reviewed: September 25, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website and our games. This site is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and controls meet WCAG AA color-contrast ratios.
  • Every image that carries information has a text description.
  • The whole site works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Pages use proper headings and landmarks for screen readers.
  • The layout works when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.
  • Motion effects are turned off if you've asked your device to reduce motion.
  • No content is hidden behind animations or scripts.

Our apps

Our iPhone games are built with Apple's native frameworks, which support VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and other iOS accessibility features. We're always working to improve how well each game supports them.

Feedback

If anything on this site or in our apps is hard to use, please tell us. Email andrewgmaxwell123@gmail.com with the page or screen and what went wrong. We'll work to fix it and to get you the information in a format that works for you.
