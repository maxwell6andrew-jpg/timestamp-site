Privacy

Wake Later · Maxwell Games · Effective November 2026

The short version

Wake Later works on your iPhone. It reads what it needs to set your alarm, decides on the phone, and doesn't send any of it to us. We don't have a server, accounts, ads, or analytics.

What the app reads, and why

  • Calendar: morning events, to find your first commitment.
  • Location (approximate, rounded to about 1 km): to check traffic with Apple Maps and weather with Apple Weather.
  • Location in the background (only if you turn on "Measure mornings"): two geofences, your home and your first stop, so the app can note when you left and arrived. No route or trail is recorded, and the times stay on your phone.
  • Texts (optional): only what you pass to the app yourself through a Shortcuts automation.

Where it goes

Nowhere. All of it is processed on your device and kept only as long as needed (messages are ignored after 24 hours). Apple Maps and Apple Weather receive an approximate location to answer requests, under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all data removes everything, including your alarms. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Wake Later isn't directed at children under 13.
