Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 3, 2026  ·  Andrew Maxwell, d/b/a Andrew Maxwell  ·  support@timestampgames.com

📷 Your photos never leave your device. TimeStamp! only reads the date each photo was taken — no images are uploaded to any server, ever.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Andrew Maxwell ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the TimeStamp! iOS app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the app works.

2. Information We Access — And Don't

Photo Library (Metadata Only)

TimeStamp! requests access to your device's photo library in order to read the timestamp metadata — the date and time each photo was taken. This is the core mechanic of the game.

  • We read the date/time a photo was captured.
  • We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any server.
  • We do not view the content of your photos.
  • All photo processing happens locally on your device.

You can revoke photo access at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → TimeStamp! Revoking access will prevent the app from functioning, but you can re-grant it at any time.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address, or contact information (unless you contact us directly)
  • Device location or GPS data
  • Your photo images or their content
  • Health or biometric data
  • Financial or payment information

3. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data. This helps us understand how the app is used — for example, which game modes are most popular and how often the app is launched. This data cannot identify you personally.

Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App session information (duration, frequency)
  • Device type and iOS version (anonymous)
  • In-app events (game mode selected, round completed)
  • Crash reports

Firebase Analytics data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Apple Game Center

TimeStamp! optionally integrates with Apple Game Center for Daily Challenge leaderboards. If you choose to participate:

  • Your Game Center display name and score are submitted to Apple's leaderboard service.
  • This integration is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • You can opt out of Game Center at any time via iOS Settings → Game Center.

We do not have access to your Apple ID or any payment information associated with your Apple account.

5. How We Use Information

The limited data we collect through Firebase Analytics is used solely to:

  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Identify and fix bugs or crashes
  • Understand which features are most valuable to players

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond improving the app.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with:

  • Firebase / Google — anonymous analytics as described above
  • Apple — Game Center leaderboard data if you opt in
  • Legal authorities — if required by law or to protect our rights

7. Children's Privacy

TimeStamp! is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, please contact us at support@timestampgames.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not store personal data on our servers, there is nothing to delete on our end. Anonymous Firebase Analytics data is retained according to Google's standard retention policies. To remove all local app data, simply delete the TimeStamp! app from your device. See our Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

9. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect any data we handle. Since your photos never leave your device and we collect no personally identifiable information, your privacy risk is minimal by design.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
